# ZORA changelog

What changed in each release of ZORA. The same version, flags and seed always make the same game,
apart from the Cosmetic settings.

## 2.0 beta 4 (2026-10-12)

### New Features

New ZORA features have been added:

- **Oops All Enemies:** Every dungeon room's enemies come from one family, picked room by room: Wizzrobes (red and blue), Keese (blue, red and dark), or Bouncers (Pols Voice and Vire). Rooms keep their enemy counts. Bosses, NPCs, traps, and Bubbles stay as they are.
- **Oops All Bosses:** Dodongos (triple or single) in every boss room of levels 1 to 8 whose layout allows them. The few rooms that can't hold Dodongos keep their own boss, and level 9 is unchanged. 

The following features from Zelda Randomizer have been re-implemented using AI-assisted clean room development:

- **Remove Most Open Stairs (RMOS)** 
- **Book To Understand NPCs**

### Changes

- The level-4 sword got a cosmetic refresh. Instead of having a somewhat strange palette that changes on many screens, it now looks like the magical sword in the white sword's colours in the A box, on the floor, in caves and shops, and when Link holds it up. In Link's hand during combat it looks like the white sword.
- The "Hopla" preset has been added now that all "Hopla" flagset features have been re-implemented in ZORA.

### Bug Fixes

-  Fixed a bug in the "Randomize Magical Sword" feature that caused extra swords to appear in every transport staircase, some of which could be picked up by the player. Learning about this bug from Custom also caused extra S-words to be said by Tetra, some of which could be heard by her neighbors.
