# ZORA changelog

What changed in each release of ZORA. The same version, flags and seed always make the same game,
apart from the Cosmetic settings.

## 2.0 beta 11 (2026-10-30)

### New Features
- **Graphics theme** (Look and feel): Off (the default) or Halloween. Halloween draws the overworld, the dungeons, the bosses and Ganon with ZeroMeaning's Halloween art from Z1M1, with Ganon's colors from it; the art follows each boss wherever the seed puts it (Randomize Boss Groups, Oops All Bosses and the rest). It changes only how the game looks: a seed plays the same and keeps its code with it on or off. It applies when a ROM is generated, not to a finished ROM. Oops All Bosses now decides whether two kept bosses share a tile by where the tiles come from rather than by their bytes, so a ROM's graphics can never change its game; no seed of the 4,000 scanned changes.
- **Swap Swords and Rings** and **Swordlish** (ZORA Extras, both Off by default; from BogieSmalls). Swap Swords and Rings (On or Random): the White Sword and the Blue Ring trade places wherever they were placed, and so do the Magical Sword and the Red Ring, the hints naming what is really there and each cave keeping its heart requirement. Swordlish (On or Random): the wooden-sword cave gives its sword only to a player holding N triforce pieces, N drawn from a range you set (1 to 8), and its old man says how many; Random picks Normal, Wood Sword Only, No Wood Sword, Swordless or Swordlish per seed. Swordlish needs Swords: Normal and removes the wood sword cave's extra candle (Extra Candles still adds the take-any cave's). Seeds without them are unchanged.
- **Item Shuffle: Random (No Vanilla)**, a sixth choice after Random: each seed shuffles its items as Item Only, Intra-Dungeon or Full, one in three each, never None. The Z1R flag string holds Item Shuffle: Random and a new ZORA flag narrows the draw, so every existing string keeps its meaning and its seeds. Extra Raft Blocks, which Random refuses with a 2nd Quest overworld and the dungeons left in place (it may draw None), works with Random (No Vanilla). The spoiler log shows the drawn value as it does for Random.
- **Mirror Overworld** (On and Random): the overworld is flipped left to right, caves and dungeons as they are; the hints name each place's region after the flip and the Dead Woods hint the path the flipped maze takes. A string with it on used to generate unmirrored with a notice. Seeds without it are unchanged.
- **Remove Maps, All Maps and All Compasses** (On and Random): no dungeon room or item cellar holds a map (nothing lies in its place, and a room that would release the map when its last foe dies opens its shutters and releases nothing), and a new save file holds every level's map, or every level's compass. Seeds without them are unchanged.
- **Shuffle Minor Dungeon Drops** (On and Random): the levels' bombs, five-rupee drops and keys join the item shuffle, so they can change level or land in the Armos, white-sword cave or coast slot (which then gives them), and an important item can land in their rooms. A key never goes to the white-sword cave. No effect with Item Shuffle None outside Mixed Quest. Nothing checks that a level keeps enough keys for its locked doors.
- **Harder Bosses** (ZORA Extras: Off, Level 1 to 3), from Z1M1 by MetalMachine: Digdogger and Manhandla move faster, Gohma fires more often, and Aquamentus fires more often and less regularly; no boss gains hit points or damage.
- **Harder Ganon** (ZORA Extras: Off, Level 1 to 3), from Z1M1 by MetalMachine: Ganon heals a little while the sword can hurt him, the time he stays visible after a hit varies around the usual, he may jump while the silver arrows can finish him, and the room he stands in shoots fireballs.
- **Offense Item Pacing** and **Defense Item Pacing** (ZORA Extras: Off, Earlier, Normal, Later), from Z1M1 by MetalMachine: the logic counts swords or rings before it counts a dungeon's items, so the swords or rings must lie where a player can get them in time (Earlier: 1 sword for levels 1 to 4 and 2 from level 5; Later: no sword asked before level 5); Normal is Z1M1's default, not ZORA's. They count sword items, not tiers (the wooden sword is one), and the blue ring counts once its shop's door is reached. A sword in the white-sword or magical-sword cave counts for Offense Item Pacing only once the starting hearts and the heart containers reachable meet the cave's requirement (the highest count the draw can give). Offense Item Pacing needs Swords: Normal, and Defense Item Pacing: Earlier needs Item Shuffle other than None or Random (Random (No Vanilla) works), since level 9 asks for both rings and the Red Ring would wait inside it.
- **Recorder Warping** (ZORA Extras, off by default), from Z1M1 by MetalMachine: it lets the logic reach a dungeon whose door stands on a screen only the raft reaches with the recorder instead (greyed out on the page unless Recorder to Unbeaten Dungeons and Recorder to New Dungeons are both on). Z1M1's Block Clipping is not ported: its clips depend on Z1M1's custom room layouts, which ZORA does not have.
- **Change Secret Spots** (On and Random): each overworld screen's secret (bombable wall, burnable tree, pushable rock or gravestone) moves along its screen, about 28 of the 54 per seed, keeping its kind; Link comes out of a moved wall's cave at the wall, and some of those screens bring their monsters in from the edges. A moved rock's, tree's or gravestone's stairs take their exit with them: Link comes out next to them.
- **Add Extra Bosses** (On and Random): each ordinary room of levels 1 to 8 gets a boss of its level's boss tier one time in four (about 49 bosses per seed against 20), in the room's own shutters and trigger; extra bosses are silent, and with Shuffle Bosses off nothing changes.
- **Blackout** (On and Random): every dungeon gets a darkened copy of level 1's colors, black floors with the walls drawn in one accent color per level, and no room is dark any more but Ganon's, so the candle is not needed to see. ZORA does not require the magical key for level 9's items.
- **Shuffle Enemy Drop Groups** (On and Random): the monster types are dealt again among the drop classes, so which monsters drop nothing, and which drop chance and drop row the others use, changes with each seed; blue Keese never drop, and class C always holds three overworld monsters and one of each dungeon enemy group.
- **Playable character** (Look and feel; it replaces the Link sprite setting), with the character and its colors together in one section and a preview of the chosen sprite in the three tunic colors:
  - ZORA's first sprite sets, by Irenepunmaster: Abobo, Agumon, Jason Voorhees, Mini-Ganon and Twilight Sparkle Humanoid Pony Edition, each shown with its artist.
  - Nine characters by ZeroMeaning, drawn for Z1M1: Ganon, Justin Bailey, Kraid, Link (Bunny), Link (Female), Link (Invisible), Mother Brain, Samus and Zelda. Where a sprite keeps the original Link's pixels (mostly his shield), those come from your own ROM.
  - Characters made from the game's own art, read from your ROM (so a ROM with custom graphics gives its own): Zelda, the Old Man, the Old Woman, the Merchant, and the Darknut, Gibdo, Goriya, Lynel, Moblin, Stalfos and Wizzrobe. Only Link changes, unless you tick a person's Swap, which also draws that person as Link.
  - Random picks from the original Link, all of these (never with the swap) and a set you loaded, so it gives a seed a different sprite than before.
- **Playable character's colors** (Look and feel): The set's own (the default) or My own. A sprite set gets the colors it was drawn for unless you pick your own: its other two colors, and its tunic colors too when its first color draws the character rather than clothing (ZeroMeaning's Ganon, Kraid, Link (Bunny), Link (Invisible), Mother Brain and Samus, and the built-in-art monsters and people). The same colors also recolor the Wood Sword, Wood Arrows, Bow, Raft, Ladder, Magical Shield, Wood Boomerang and Zelda, which the game draws in the character's palette. A new advanced setting, Playable character's other colors, sets the character's two colors besides the tunic. Picking a tunic or other color switches the choice to My own, and saved settings that already had colors of their own keep them.
- **Low-health beep** (Look and feel): a fourth value, Quarter speed: the warning beep sounds a quarter as often (every 76 frames, about 1.3 s). Random still draws only Removed or Kept, and Hidden Health still silences it.
- **Music:** with no track tagged Title in the library (the standard library has none), the title screen plays the game's own title music instead of an overworld track, and the track list says so; an Overworld track pinned to the title screen is warned about as not tagged for it. The playlist editor has an Only show tagged tracks checkbox: each area's list shows only the tracks tagged for it, and any track already pinned there.

### Changes
- **Gatekeeper Bosses is retired.** A boss pass added to finished dungeons kept breaking rules the earlier stages rely on; a gate-based dungeon style will be designed from scratch for a later major release. ZORA flag strings with it on (Anywhere, Late or Early) still load, and now build without gatekeepers: the same seed as the same flags with it off. The option is gone from the page.
- **Hints:** three random hint texts are retired and two community texts join the pool, both from ultralavos: the door repairer's WOULD YOU KINDLY GIVE ME SOME RUPEES and the bomb upgrader's YOU'RE GONNA NEED A BIGGER BOMB BAG. The door repairer's, the bomb upgrader's and slot 16's pools change size, so most seeds draw different hint texts (and so get a different seed code); nothing else in a seed changes.
- **Music:** Random no longer draws a song that ends instead of looping. Such a song stopped partway through an area and could leave one note held until the music changed (ISUMatt's level 9 with "Tecmo Super Bowl - Unknown Song"); 1 song of the standard library and 64 of Rex's list do this. You can still pick one in the playlist, and the page then warns that it doesn't loop. Seeds with Music: Random may draw different tracks.
- **Add Money or Life Rooms on with Change "Leave your..." Rooms off** is now produced: the merchants keep the original game's wares and payments (a heart container or 50 rupees) and show their own text, a drawn line from slot 27's quotes (the original game's money-or-life line under Normal hints, an empty box under Blank). With Change "Leave your..." Rooms off, slot 27 now always draws its own text, so seeds with it off may change. Both settings at Random are now separate coins: merchants without a new toll come out in about a quarter of the seeds with both at Random, so their seeds may change.
- **Ending credits:** the Randomizer Staff box adds ZeroMeaning under Sprite Design, Gromfalloon for the website logo, and MetalMachine under Additional Technical Contributions. Every ROM's credits change; nothing else does.
- **The page stops Generate after 15 s.** With the seed the page chose, it quietly tries new seeds within that time; with a seed you typed or a shared link's, it says the seed took too long and to try another. A seed that finishes is unchanged.
- **Level 9 Item 1 or 2** at the Red Ring is refused with Defense Item Pacing: Earlier, and at the White Sword with any Offense Item Pacing value: level 9 asks for two rings or two swords, which the pinned item inside level 9 can never be, so those seeds ran to the generation limit before.

### Bug Fixes
- **Statistics screen:** a level's row now shows the number the status bar shows in that level. In second-quest and mixed dungeons some levels show another level's number (in a second-quest seed, levels 2 and 3, 4 and 5, and 7 and 8 trade numbers), and the screen labeled each by its own, so those rows read swapped (their times were right); with Hide dungeon numbers on, levels 1 to 8 now read "?" as in play. The levels entered and not finished are listed in the order of their shown numbers. Reported on beta 10. Every ROM's statistics screen bytes change; nothing else does.
- **Level word in the hints:** with a level word shorter than LEVEL (KEEP, LAIR, DEN and the like, chosen or drawn by Random), a hint naming a level could run past the text box's right edge and wrap round to its left ("KEEP-5 LIES HIGH IN DE" / "ATH MOUNTAIN."), or never end and show part of the next hint. The game skips the blank padding tile that closed such a line, and with it the line break; the padding now ends with a space, and every text ZORA writes now closes its lines the same way. The level word is a player setting, so only ROMs with such a word change.
- **Hint style: Blank:** a person with a blank hint drew one black square on a dungeon floor where the text starts; nothing is drawn now. Blank seeds change and no other hint style does.
- **The hungry Goriya's room** always has the black floor of a person room: when Allow the dungeon start room to move swapped the start room with the Goriya's room, the Goriya took the old start room's floor color. Only seeds where that swap happened change.
- **Dungeon Quest with Move Start Rooms:** when a second-quest level 9's start room moved, the logic still looked for its gate at the original room, found the real gate room's shutters closed and could treat level 9 as shut. It now uses the room above the start room, where the gate person stands, in every quest, so seeds with a second-quest level 9 and Move Start Rooms on may change.
- **Defense Item Pacing with Shop Items in the Item Pool:** the blue ring, which joins the pool, now counts toward the pace, so Earlier builds in a second or two instead of running into the generation limit (every seed timed out before). Seeds with that pair of settings change; every other seed is unchanged.
