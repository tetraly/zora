# ZORA changelog

What changed in each release of ZORA. The same version, flags and seed always make the same game,
apart from the Cosmetic settings.

## Unreleased

### New Features
- **Item Counter** (ZORA extras, Miscellaneous; beta: On, Off or Random; Off by default; idea by Ultralavos): the HUD's empty row under the rupees shows a chest and how many of the area's major items you have found out of its total, "1/3" in a level and "4/6" on the overworld. A level counts its rooms and item cellars; the overworld counts the white sword cave, the Armos and coast items, the magical sword, letter and wood sword caves while their shuffles are on, and the shop wares Shop Items in the Item Pool and Shuffle Blue Potion add. Only what the item shuffle deals counts: under Item Only, Within Each Dungeon and None the bosses' heart containers do not (the coast's does, wherever it lands); Triforce pieces, take-any caves and re-buyable wares never do. Totals over 9 show in hexadecimal (A = 10). Not offered in Archipelago while in beta. With it off, ROMs are unchanged.
- **Wizzrobes Shoot Wallmasters** (Advanced tab, beta: On, Off or Random; Off by default; idea by Ultralavos; not with Oops All Enemies: Wizzrobes): a Wizzrobe's magic shot is a Wallmaster's hand. It flies over blocks and vanishes at the walls as the magic does. When it hits Link it deals a Wallmaster's damage (half a heart) and takes him back to the level's entrance, as a Wallmaster's grab does, keeping his hearts; a hit that empties his hearts kills him. The Magical Shield blocks it and it vanishes. Link's wand is unchanged, and on the overworld the shot stays magic. The hand is drawn with the Wallmaster's tiles, so it turns Shuffle Enemy Groups on, and the deal puts the Wizzrobes in a dungeon group (never the overworld's) with the Wallmasters. Not offered in Archipelago while in beta. With it off, ROMs are unchanged; the seed document and spoiler log list it among the ZORA flags.

## 2.0.0 (2026-11-08)

ZORA 2.0.0 is the first full release. A few settings are still in beta: they read "(beta)" on the page and stay locked until Enable Beta Flags is on. In 2.0.0 they are Universal Drops v2.0 and Spoiler Log Detail Level's Basic, Medium and Full, with the tracker file that comes with a log, and any Random that can pick one of them.

### New Features
- **Enable Beta Flags** (above the tabs, in yellow): settings still in beta, and any Random that can pick one, are locked until this switch is on, with a warning that they are less tested. Flags that use one turn it on when pasted or loaded, and bug report info lists the beta settings in use. Shown only while some setting is beta; never part of a flag string, so it never changes a seed.
- **Universal Drops v2.0** (ZORA Extras, beta: On, Off or Random; Off by default; never with Allow Universal Drops In Shapes): in Shapes dungeons each level holds as many bombs, rupees and keys as without universal drops, spread over every room that can hold one; a room that can hold an item at its center puts it there half the time, and otherwise at a random one of its spots. Each level's three other item spots are drawn per seed among squares reachable from every door. With it off, ROMs are unchanged; the seed document and spoiler log list it among the ZORA flags.
- **Spoiler Log Detail Level** (Misc tab, in place of the Print Quest Info switch): None (the default, no log), Basic, Medium, Full or Random. Every level but None is beta.
  - **Basic** (beta) is Print Quest Info on. Its log holds what the original randomizer's log holds: the seed, level 9's entry rule, the start screen, each major item's place (a level's by its quest, number and entrance screen), every cave entrance by screen, the shops' wares and prices, the money secrets' amounts, and a map of each level with its passages, one-way passages, way out and staircase letters.
  - **Medium** (beta) adds, for each major item (Heart Containers and triforce pieces too), whether it lies on the floor or down a stairway and the room it's in, and marks each item stairway on the maps.
  - **Full** (beta) adds every room of each level: its layout, push block, monsters, floor item, stairway and the kind of door on each side.
  - **Random** (beta) picks Basic, Medium or Full, one in three each, so a seed with Random always has a log.
  - Medium and Full turn on a new ZORA flag, **Detailed Spoiler Log** (beta), which the page sets for you. Archipelago doesn't offer the setting: a YAML's print_quest_info is accepted and ignored.
- **Download tracker file** (beta), beside "Spoiler log" for a seed with a log: a ZoraTracker save file at the log's level (Basic: the overworld, the item boxes and each level's rooms, walls, passages, entrance and staircase pairs; Medium adds item cellars and major floor items; Full adds Ganon's and Zelda's rooms, people, named layouts, door colors, monsters and minor floor items). ZTracker opens it too, after a version warning that "Attempt Load" gets past, and the page names what ZTracker won't show for that seed (such as the Wood Sword and Magical Sword caves' items). A seed made with Encode level data and a log level gets its log and tracker file too; the Visualizer stays off for it.
- **Number of seeds** (beside Generate, single player): up to 100 seeds at once in one .zip, or, in Chrome and Edge, up to 1000 saved straight into a folder you pick. The seeds count up from the seed box (123, 124, 125, ...), and each ROM is the same as generating that seed alone. The download holds each seed's spoiler log and tracker file when the settings make them. The page shows progress and the time left; Stop keeps the seeds already made, and a seed that fails is listed while the rest carry on. The count is remembered in this browser and never goes in a flag string or link. Seeds are unchanged.
- **A player FAQ** ([docs/faq.md](https://github.com/tetraly/zora/blob/main/docs/faq.md)), linked from the page's header with the [ZORA Discord](https://discord.gg/zescSjDtme), left of Flag guide.

### Changes
- **A seed made with a spoiler log is a different game, on purpose**: at Spoiler Log Detail Level Basic, Medium or Full (or Random, which always lands on one) a seed has different items and icons from beta 12's seed of the same number, and each level gives its own seed, so a log can't be made afterwards for a no-log race seed. With None, seeds are unchanged.
- **Flag strings with Print Quest Info at Random** (beta 12's included) now always give a log, at Basic, Medium or Full; before, one seed in two had none.
- **A flag string with Print Quest Info on** (beta 12's included) turns on Enable Beta Flags when pasted or loaded, since Basic is now beta.
- **The spoiler log at Basic** no longer lists the settings, the texts, the seed code, the requirements or the item rooms.
- **The settings follow the original Z1R program's layout** (issue #15): Overworld Quest, Dungeon Quest and Hint Type sit above the tabs; the Z1R settings move to the program's tabs (Shops, Dungeon Items, Dungeons, Monsters, Misc, Overworld, Start Items, then ZORA extras, Advanced and Look and feel), two columns each, in the program's order; ZORA extras are grouped as Items, Locations, Enemies and Miscellaneous. Each min/max pair (sword hearts, Triforce Range, Swordlish pieces) is one line. Rows of buttons drop "(beta)" from their values so they fit, and the pinned-item dropdowns drop their "No item pinned" note. Flag strings, shared links and settings files are unchanged. Seeds are unchanged.
- **The beta notice** at the top of the page shows only while Enable Beta Flags is on, and asks for bug reports in the ZORA Discord with the text from "Copy bug report info". Seeds are unchanged.
- **The opening story** no longer tells you what level 9 requires; it reads the same on every seed. The old man at level 9's entrance still tells you.
