# ZORA changelog

What changed in each release of ZORA. The same version, flags and seed always make the same game,
apart from the Cosmetic settings.

## 2.0 beta 8 (2026-10-23)

- New settings, each On or Random: Maximum Enemy Health (every ordinary monster at full hit points; the level 9 Vire cap still applies), Increase Bomb Capacity Upgrade (the bomb upgrade adds 2 more bombs), Killable Drop Items (only Link picks up dropped items; his weapons destroy them), Hidden Numbers (rupees, keys, bombs, prices and death counts show "?"; the boss-sound label stays), Rupees Affect Speed (Link walks faster the more rupees he holds; with Pegasus Boots the faster speed wins) and Prevent Clipping.
- Coast Item: Non-Heart: the coast always holds an item other than a heart container (it could keep its heart container).
- Level 9 Item 1 and 2 can now be the Bow, Ladder, Power Bracelet, Raft or Recorder, or the Wand, with Allow Important Items in 9 on (refused with it off; at Random it is on). Archipelago holds these values and Coast Item: Non-Heart back until their logic scan.
- Shuffle Bomb Upgraders can be Random, and so can Add Money or Life Rooms and Change Leave Your Rooms together: Random never adds merchants without changing their toll. Encode level data pasted at Random is read as off (it was refused).
- Extra Raft Blocks is refused with a 2nd Quest overworld, Item Shuffle None, dungeons left in place and a Dungeon Quest that keeps the raft in level 3 (Random included): the raft sat behind its own raft-blocked entrance and generation gave up after 2,000 attempts. A Random Extra Raft Blocks turns off in those seeds.
- Seed code pictures (on the file-select screen and on the page): only items drawn in Link's colors in play (the wooden sword, arrow, bow, raft, ladder, magical shield and boomerang) take the tunic color; every other item is drawn in red, orange and white, so the magical sword no longer looks like the wooden sword, the heart no longer disappears with a black tunic, and the heart and Triforce show in one steady color.
- Add L4 Sword: the level 4 sword never stands on a block, statue or staircase (it could on level 9's diamond-staircase room's stairs); generation now rejects any item on such a square.
- Level 9: a hint person below a life-or-money room never has a door on the wall behind them, which Link could not reach past the person.
- Low-health beep (Look and feel): a third value, Half speed: the warning beep sounds half as often (tetraly/zora#3). The default stays Removed, and Hidden Health still silences it.
- Archipelago now offers Level 9 Entry's every value, the Uphill Battle settings' Off and Random (Force Ganon Fight, Add 2nd Quest Rooms, Shuffle Dungeon Monsters, Shuffle Ganon and Zelda, Shuffle Level 9 Monsters), Dungeon Room Shuffle Vanilla and Random, and Recorder to Unbeaten Dungeons, Make Important Items Drops, Add 2nd Quest Doors and Add 2nd Quest Monsters, On and Random. Replace Book Fire with Explosion stays Off in Archipelago: it is not compatible.
- Statistics screen: a level 4 sword (Add L4 Sword) shows as it does in play, the magical sword in the white sword's blue; it showed red.
- Do Not Segment Dungeons (ZORA Extras, On or Random, off by default): shapes dungeons are never split into segments; walls open until every room can be reached through doors, and the stairways become shortcuts. Dungeons from the 1st or 2nd Quest keep their stairway areas. Archipelago offers it, On and Random.
- Progressive Items: a cave that gives its item away (the wooden-sword, white-sword, magical-sword, letter and take-any caves) hides an upgrade you already hold at its top level, as shops do, so a useless pickup can't use up a take-one cave's one take.
- Progressive Items works with Extra Candles on (tetraly/zora#13): the two candles are candle upgrades like any other. With Progressive Items on, a Random Extra Candles flips its own coin; it was always off.
- Red Bubble Curse: Slow Speed no longer turns the Pegasus Boots off: with both on, the boots run as usual and the curse halves Link's speed, running too, while it lasts.
- Empty texts (every person under Hint Type: Blank, and the few empty texts other seeds have) show an empty box; a stray mark showed at the start of each.
- Item Sprites in an Archipelago game with the logo: no item takes the logo's picture, and other players' items keep the logo.
- Pegasus Boots: the file saved after the ending holds the Magical Boots, so you can run through the seed again faster.
- When Vires are the only monsters in level 9 that can drop bombs, they have at most 4 hit points, so a magical-sword hit or a bomb kills one outright and bombs can still drop there (a Vire that survives a hit splits into Keese, which drop nothing).
- View in visualizer works with Pegasus Boots on: the visualizer refused those seeds. The seed document is now in seed format 1.1, which names the Pegasus Boots and Magical Boots ("Boots Upgrade" with Progressive Items).
- Two presets: TTP5: Uphill Battle and SGLive Onsite 2026, the 2026 tournament strings as the original randomizer's v3.6.6 builds them in (TTP5: Muffle Rug and Pick 5 already were). Copy race info names the Race ROM switch (Encode level data): on, no spoiler log; off, a spoiler log is available.
- Hint Type: Deception (helpful hints, each fact true or false with equal chance), Blank (every person says nothing, but the toll and the level 9 entrance) and Random (any of the six). Random% now generates.
- Change Hidden OW Tiles: On and Random (the overworld's secret rocks, walls, trees and gravestones look marked; with the first or second quest, the second-quest-only ones stay plain). Item Sprites: Fun Percentage, Sprite Shuffle and Random; with them the file-select screen, the page and the spoiler log show the seed code as four characters instead of item pictures.
- Add 2nd Quest Doors and Add 2nd Quest Monsters: On and Random. Doors take the second quest's weights too (walk-through walls appear); second-quest monsters join the shuffles, and ropes and Stalfos take their second-quest form.
- Replace Book Fire with Explosion: On and Random. With the Book, the wand's shot ends in a bomb blast; the Book stays among the shuffled items and the shops keep the magic shield, and B47 works beside any Book pin. Coast Item: any item but the Ladder and Non-Heart.
- The rest of the Uphill Battle settings, each On or Random: Recorder to Unbeaten Dungeons (the whirlwind visits the levels whose Triforce piece you lack), Hide Dungeon Numbers ("LEVEL-?" in levels 1 to 8), Hidden Health (the hearts drawn in a dark colour and no low-health tone, whatever the player settings say), Make Important Items Drops (fifteen items join the enemy drop table), Randomize Item Drop Rate and Maximum Boss Health (every boss at full hit points; under Swordless the wooden-sword cave sells the red candle). Red Bubble Curse: Inverted Controls, Slow Speed and Random (the red bubble no longer takes the sword away).
- Dungeon Room Shuffle: Off and Random. Off leaves vanilla dungeons' rooms in place; with Force Ganon Fight, Shuffle Dungeon Start Room or Shuffle the Hungry Goriya on, each seed either turns the room shuffle on (within each dungeon) or turns those off. Shuffle Wood Sword Cave: Random. Print Quest Info: On and Random (no effect on the ROM).
- Set Ganon HP to 0 and Maximum Boss Health cannot both be on, and Replace Book Fire with Explosion cannot be on with the Book pinned at the Armos, the coast or the white-sword cave; when random, a seed never combines them. The spoiler log shows these the way it shows the coins.
- A flag string with Mirrored Overworld on or Random is accepted: the seed is generated unmirrored, and the page and the spoiler log say so ("Mirrored overworld isn't supported yet; this seed is not mirrored"). The page still offers only Off.
- A "Copy race info" button beside the other copy buttons: the version, the seed, the flag strings, the seed code's item names and whether a spoiler log can be made, in one short block for a race channel.
- The spoiler log's flag list shows each setting's final value when a later rule changed it (the cave shuffle and monster shuffle coins, Progressive Items' Extra Candles rule, the ZORA Extras' rules), after its Random result: "? -> Off -> On (monster shuffle coin)".
- Hint Type: Normal: the original game's texts, except the level 9 entrance text (what Level 9 Entry asks for) and the money-or-life toll text, and, with Randomize Lost Hills or Dead Woods, the maze's hint-shop text and its 1-rupee price. The white-sword cave and the hint shops keep their original texts and prices; Shuffle Dungeon Text exchanges the original dungeon texts. Archipelago offers it.
- The Uphill Battle settings, each Off or Random: Shuffle Level 9 Monsters (level 9 keeps its own monsters and sprites; the Wizzrobes stay in its group), Shuffle Ganon and Zelda (both stay in the rooms the shapes gave them), Force Ganon Fight (nothing walls Zelda's room behind Ganon's; Ganon must still be reachable and beatable), Shuffle Dungeon Monsters (each room keeps its monster; with Shuffle Monsters Between Levels or Shuffle Ganon and Zelda on, each seed either turns it on or turns those off), Add 2nd Quest Rooms (first-quest room layouts only) and Shuffle Overworld Group (overworld enemies stay on the overworld). Archipelago offers only Shuffle Overworld Group's for now.
- Level 9 Entry, every value: 7 down to 1 triforces (any levels' pieces), Open Level 9, Triforce Range (a number of pieces drawn from Triforce Range (min) to (max)), Specific Triforces (that many drawn levels' pieces), Other Item (a drawn item instead of pieces: the sword, the recorder, a candle, an arrow, the bow, the magical key, the raft, the ladder, the wand, the book or the power bracelet, never the letter) and Random (8 triforces, Open, Triforce Range, Specific Triforces or Other Item). The person at level 9's entrance says what it asks for; no hint does. Starting Triforce Pieces count toward a number of pieces, and toward Specific Triforces only when they are chosen levels' pieces. Level 9 Entrance = Level 4 sword (ZORA Extras) needs Level 9 Entry at 8 triforces. Not offered in Archipelago yet.
- Level 9 Entry: Specific Triforces' entrance text uses the level word you choose ("YOU NEED THE TRIFORCE PIECES OF PALACES 2 6 8."), and the spoiler log states what level 9's entrance asks for: the number of pieces, the chosen levels or the item, and what Random resolved to.
- Music, randomized (test; docs/music.md): the look-and-feel setting Music has two new values, Random (standard library), tracks from Quantam's FamiTracker library downloaded from his repository, and Random (local library), tracks from library files you choose (Zelda II Randomizer's libraries too). The title screen, the overworld, each level and the ending and credits get their own track; with them come Streaming-safe tracks only (on by default) and a playlist that pins songs to any area, the game's own songs too. The statistics screen keeps its numbers through resets and turning the console off, as without music (Quantam's lozft of 2026-10-22).
- New ZORA flag, Disable Custom Music (Off by default): for races, Music can then be Off or On only, since randomized music changes how often the game lags a little. It changes nothing in the seed itself. Not offered in Archipelago.
