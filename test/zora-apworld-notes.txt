- Highlight No-Drop Rooms (bug report, 2026-10-24): a room that can never hold an item now shows its blocks, water, statues and stairs in red ($16) in every level, entrance rooms and water rooms included (the first version skipped them), and a room that can drop, its walls and its doors look as the level always looked. A vanilla moat that can drop shows the level's block color rather than its water color. Rooms in the person-room and sand palettes keep their colors. The dark-room fade fades the red.

- Gatekeeper Bosses: the rooms the gatekeeper's item and a moved compass leave no longer drop an item when cleared (they showed a magical sword, or a second Triforce of Power); their shutters still open on the kill.
- New ZORA option, Gatekeeper Bosses (Off, Anywhere, Late, Early): in each of levels 1 to 8, as in the original game, one boss room is the only way to the part of the level holding the triforce. Its shutters open when the boss falls, and its item (the heart container, or what the item shuffle put in its place) appears then. The compass is always in front of the boss. Anywhere puts any share of the level behind the boss, Late puts the boss close to the triforce (at most a quarter of the level behind it), Early puts at least half the level, mostly more, behind it. The rooms beside the boss roar with its own sound (Dodongo and Gohma; Digdogger, Manhandla and Patra), never Ganon's. Off by default; not yet offered in Archipelago.

THEN ZELDA RANDOMIZER TOTALLY CHANGED HYRULE ..."), laid out as the original's page (justified, the last two lines centered), with NINTENDO in red, ZELDA RANDOMIZER in green and ZORA in blue; its last two lines name the seed's level 9 requirement, what there is to find in red (the triforce count, the chosen levels, the item, an open level 9 or the four swords, with the player's level word); the note Link holds after the item list now says "PLEASE LOOK UP ZORA.GG FOR DETAILS.", in the note's own letters and colors, so a graphics pack's note carries through (one whose note is drawn otherwise keeps it).

- Look and feel: Random for the tunic colors (from eleven colors, two Random tunics never alike), the heart color (from seven), the low-health beep (removed or kept) and the level name (one of the listed words), each picked by the seed number, so the same seed always looks the same; Archipelago's player settings offer them too.
- Look and feel: Link sprite: load a Link sprite set (a .json file with a name, an author and 640 bytes of tile art; docs/link-sprite-sets.md) to redraw Link in play, on the file-select screen and in the title story, or Random between it and the original Link. ZORA ships no sets yet.

- Music: Random (local library): a track's use that The Legend of Zelda has no area for (another game's, such as Refights) no longer stops the library from loading: that use is left out with a warning, and so is a track with no other use; files with no track to place are refused in plain words when chosen, before Generate.
