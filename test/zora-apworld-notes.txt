ZORA 2.0.2 brings Archipelago's item messages in the status bar (from TheGobOne), more of the ZORA extras in Archipelago (Bomb Upgrades, Triforces and Maps/Compasses in the Item Pool, DeathLink, Item Counter, Wizzrobes Shoot Wallmasters), a way to build your Archipelago ROM on the page, and fixes for Offense Item Pacing with the joined swords and for Extra Raft Blocks' cave colors (fixed by TheGobOne). Against 2.0.1, 20 of the release check's 40 seed ROMs and all 476 of its matrix ROMs differ, because every ROM shows the new version number; 428 of its 476 seed documents differ (seed documents are format 1.2).

### New Features

- **Archipelago: item messages in the status bar** (from TheGobOne). The client shows each item you send or receive in the blank top row of the status bar, ten seconds each (five while several wait), says when items wait for you to leave a cave or cellar, and accepts ROMs with Music: Random. Archipelago ROMs color that row white so a long message never turns red over -LIFE-; single-player ROMs are unchanged.
- **Bomb Upgrades in the Item Pool** (ZORA extras, Items; Multiworld only; beta; Off by default; ZORA string version 23). The two bomb upgraders become locations: each shows the item placed with him where his rupee lay and sells it once for his price, and his own Bomb Upgrade goes into the pool, raising your bomb capacity by the seed's upgrade amount wherever it is found. Offered in Archipelago (its logic scan built 200 of 200 seeds). The flags spec's tracker hints (`docs/flags-api/zora-flags.json`) name the two sellers as places.
- **Add Triforces to the Item Pool and Add Maps/Compasses to the Item Pool** (ZORA extras, Items; beta: On, Off or Random; Off by default; ZORA string version 23). Each level's Triforce piece, or its map and compass, is an item of its own ("Level 3 Triforce", "Level 3 Map"), shuffled with the other items as far as Item Shuffle reaches: within its own level under Within each dungeon, into any level, cave or shop under Dungeon items only and Full. It counts for its own level wherever you find it, and shows that level's number beside it. Taking a piece refills your hearts and the level goes on. Level 9 opens on the pieces it asks for, wherever they were found. The Recorder's whirlwind (with Recorder to New Dungeons too) visits a level once its piece is held, Recorder to Unbeaten Dungeons counts that level as beaten, and the statistics screen stamps its time when its piece is taken, wherever it was found. No effect with Item Shuffle: None. In Archipelago, the items can go into any world. How co-op (z1rr-coop) shares a piece taken outside its level is untested.
- **DeathLink in Archipelago** (Archipelago's own `death_link` option, off by default; DeathLink: On on the page's Multiworld mode). When Link runs out of hearts, every other DeathLink player dies too, and when one of them dies, so does Link, as soon as he is in normal play: never in a cave, a scroll, the item screen or the Triforce fanfare, and never after Zelda is rescued. A received death plays the game's own death and counts on the statistics screen; it is never sent back. A death that arrives on the title or file-select screen, or while Link is already dying, is dropped. A sent death names the level Link was in. It needs no change to the ROM. With OHKO it is harsh, but it works.

### Changes

- **Wizzrobes Shoot Wallmasters in Archipelago:** the world now offers it (`wizzrobes_shoot_wallmasters`: off, on or random). Beta settings may now be offered in Archipelago.
- **Archipelago: settings waiting for their logic scan are now beta settings you can choose.** On the Multiworld tab, with Enable Beta Flags on, you can now pick Universal Drops v2.0, Add Extra Bosses, Harder Bosses, Harder Ganon, Swap Swords and Rings, and Level 9 Item 1 and 2 at the Ladder, the Power Bracelet, the Raft or the Recorder, and the ZORA world accepts them. They have not passed ZORA's logic scan for multiworld, so they could make generation fail or make the multiworld unbeatable, which would affect the other players' games too: the tab shows a big warning while one is in use, and the exported YAML starts with it. Archipelago's own "random" never picks one. Swords: No Wood Sword, Swordless and Random and Swordlish stay out of Archipelago.
- **Item Counter in Archipelago** (a beta setting on the Multiworld tab, with the same warning). An Archipelago game's counter counts every location of yours, including the ones holding another player's item, but Shuffle Minor Dungeon Drops' rooms, as in single player. ZORA-mode seeds are unchanged.
- **Build your Archipelago ROM from your .apzora on the page, with your own look and feel.** The Multiworld tab has a new step: give it the .apzora you got from the host and your Zelda ROM, change your cosmetic settings (button options, colors, sprite, level word, music on or off and so on) and download your ROM, named as Archipelago would name it. Only how your game looks and sounds changes; the items, your slot and every other player's game are the same, so nobody has to re-roll. A patch made by another ZORA version isn't built: the page says which version made it and links that version's page.
- **Load an Archipelago YAML on the Multiworld tab.** A new button reads a ZORA YAML back into the page, so you can change it or make a new one from it: its options, player name, look-and-feel settings and DeathLink are filled in. Anything the page can't use (an unknown setting, a value Archipelago doesn't offer, a line it can't read) is listed with its line or name, and the rest still loads. Exported YAMLs now also list both flag strings at the top, ready to paste into the flag boxes.
- **The Multiworld tab no longer shows the settings that are always off in Archipelago** (Encode Level Data, Item Shuffle and its Random (No Vanilla), Print Quest Info, Shuffle Wood Sword, Disable Custom Music, Detailed Spoiler Log). A shared link or settings file that sets one no longer blocks the export: a note says its value is ignored. YAMLs that name them still load.
- **Archipelago: a shop ware holding another player's item is priced by how important the item is.** A progression item costs 60-80 rupees, as an item you may need does; a useful or filler item costs 30-40. It used to keep the ware's own price. The rest of the seed's prices are as before. The recipe in an Archipelago patch is now format 1.3, which carries each other player's item's class, so a patch made by an older .apworld needs that .apworld to rebuild its ROM.
- **Archipelago: the ZORA world can take Shuffle Minor Dungeon Drops' bombs, 5 rupees and keys as locations.** With it on, every level room or stairway holding one is a location, the keys stay in your own world, and 5 Rupees is an item that can be sent to you. Archipelago offers Shuffle Minor Dungeon Drops On and Random. Seeds without it, and every ZORA-mode seed, are unchanged; the Item Counter never counts these rooms.
- **Archipelago ROMs: room for new item codes.** An Archipelago game's ROM can hold item codes beyond the game's own in dungeon rooms (a sixth bit) and draw and give them; to make room, it moves one of the game's tables (the one that starts each kind of enemy) out of the always-loaded part of the ROM. No seed uses the new codes yet, so play is unchanged.
- **Archipelago: another player's item always shows the Archipelago logo, colored by how important it is** (red for progression, blue for useful, your tunic's color for filler), in rooms, caves and shops alike; picking it up gives you nothing. The setting "Other players' items look like" is retired: a YAML that still sets it works, and the setting is ignored. The shops' magical shields are no longer turned into fairies.
- **Seed documents are format 1.2**, which adds the item names "Archipelago Item (Progression)", "(Useful)" and "(Filler)" for another player's item in an Archipelago game's ROM, and "Level N Triforce", "Level N Map" and "Level N Compass" for a level's own piece, map and compass when they are in the item pool.
- **Archipelago: a game with Offense Item Pacing no longer sometimes fails to build its ROM.** ZORA's final check that your world can be finished counted only the heart containers it had placed itself in the extra caves and shop wares, not the ones the multiworld put there, so a sword behind a heart requirement could look out of reach. It now counts what your world really holds. ROMs that built before are unchanged.
- **Archipelago: a room that only looks like a person's room no longer shuts off the rooms past it.** A Gleeok or trap room with the Black room's layout and the monster flag was read as a person's room, whose way north is barred, so in some second-quest seeds the logic put its Triforce and the rooms past it out of reach. The game blocks Link only behind a real person, and the logic now does too. ZORA-mode seeds are unchanged.
- **Archipelago: a seed whose progression item sits in Zelda's room is no longer refused.** With Shuffle Minor Dungeon Drops on, a progression item can be dealt to an enemy room that Zelda's room later moves into. That room is never a location, so the item stays where ZORA put it, after Ganon. FINISH's check lost track of it and refused the seed. It now finds the item in Zelda's room, as ZORA's own check did. ZORA-mode seeds are unchanged.
- **Race bots can roll ten more presets.** `https://zora.gg/presets.json` (revision 2) now also lists the presets on the flag page that are a Z1R flag string alone, each with an empty ZORA string, after the seven it already held: Consternation (`consternation`), Consternation Remastered (`remastered`), Hopla (`hopla`), Consternation without level encoding (`consternation-noenc`), Vanilla (`vanilla`), OHK0HPla Remastered (`ohk0hpla`), TTP5: Muffle Rug (`mufflerug`), TTP5: Pick 5 (`pick5`), TTP5: Uphill Battle (`uphill`) and SGLive Onsite 2026 (`sglive2026`). Random% stays out, as it stays off the menu. Seeds are unchanged.
- **Shuffle Minor Dungeon Drops never puts a key in the White Sword Cave.** A key from a room whose item byte also carried another bit could get past the check and land there. Seeds with Shuffle Minor Dungeon Drops on can change; every other seed is unchanged.
- **Music: Random ROMs: the music engine's code sits in different free spots of the fixed bank.** New Archipelago code took some of the space it used, so a few of its pieces moved. The music sounds the same; only Music: Random ROMs' bytes change.
- **Archipelago: generation no longer fails when the Magical Sword is a player's second sword.** With Randomize Magical Sword and Offense Item Pacing on, Archipelago could place the Magical Sword where it opens the levels that ask for two swords; ZORA's own check did not count that sword and stopped everyone's generation. It now counts every sword where it lies. Seeds are unchanged.
- **Randomize Magical Sword and Add L4 Sword in Level 2 with Offense Item Pacing: the generator now counts that sword wherever it lands.** It used to judge these seeds as if Link had one sword fewer, so it turned down good layouts and its level pacing did not match the swords you can really get. Seeds with these settings (Offense Item Pacing on with Randomize Magical Sword, or with Add L4 Sword: Level 2) differ from 2.0.1's for the same flags and seed number; every other seed is unchanged.
- **Randomize Magical Sword with Offense Item Pacing: the Magical Sword Cave never asks for hearts from levels you can't enter yet.** The generator counted heart containers in levels pacing opens only with two swords, so a seed could need those hearts to get the second sword (in 2.0.1, seed 142 with Offense Item Pacing: Earlier, and seed 196 with Normal). Those seeds could still be beaten, but only by entering levels with fewer swords than pacing promises. A few seeds with these settings differ from 2.0.1's; every other seed is unchanged.
- **Archipelago with Offense Item Pacing: generation no longer fails when a sword cave asks fewer hearts than it could.** ZORA's check before patching assumed each sword cave asked the most hearts it can be given (14 for the Magical Sword Cave), while Archipelago's logic used what the cave really asks. With Add L4 Sword: Level 2 or Randomize Magical Sword, the check could then refuse a placement Archipelago had made and stop everyone's generation. It now asks the same hearts as the game. Seeds are unchanged.
- **Extra Raft Blocks: cave text is white again** (fixed by TheGobOne). Every cave takes its colors from overworld screen $44, and Extra Raft Blocks paints part of $44 brown, so every cave's text came out brown, in single-player and Archipelago ROMs alike. Caves now take their colors from a screen with the original ones. ROMs with Extra Raft Blocks differ from 2.0.1's in that one byte; nothing else changes.
- **Copy seed code now copies Discord emoji names.** "Copy seed code", and the "Seed code:" line of "Copy bug report info" and "Copy race info", write each item as its emoji name in race servers, with no spaces ("MagicalKey, WoodArrow, HeartContainer, PowerBracelet"); the Pegasus Boots are "PegasusBoots". The page still shows the item names beside the icons, as screen readers read them. Under Item Sprites other than normal the four characters are copied as before. ROMs are unchanged.

### Fixes

- **Archipelago: HUD messages could garble the status bar and minimap in rooms that lag** (found in testing by wolf2k). In a room with many moving sprites the game sometimes needs more than a frame, and a message written then landed in the middle of the game's own status-bar update: the message showed stray digits and hearts, and the status bar, the minimap and some of the game's tiles turned to garbage. The client now writes item messages, received items and DeathLink deaths only between the game's frames, so in those rooms they can wait a moment longer. ROMs are unchanged.
- **Archipelago: Shuffle Minor Dungeon Drops' bombs, rupees and keys stay out of the rooms the Item Counter counts** (found in testing by wolf2k). Archipelago could put your own bombs, 5 rupees and keys in a level's counted rooms and your Bow or Triforce in the uncounted bomb, rupee and key rooms, so a level's count no longer told you what was left. In Archipelago, Shuffle Minor Dungeon Drops now means your bomb, rupee and key rooms trade filler with the multiworld: ZORA no longer shuffles those items itself (so no cave or shop holds one), each bomb, rupee or key room holds only filler, yours or another player's, and no other location holds your own bombs, 5 rupees or keys (another world can still hold your bombs and 5 rupees). Single-player Shuffle Minor Dungeon Drops is unchanged.
- **Archipelago: item messages in caves and cellars show right away** (reported by TheGobOne). In a cave, a "SENT ..." line could wait up to two seconds, longest while the cave's text was typing and with the Item Counter on, because the client only wrote on the frames the game was redrawing the status bar. It now writes on any frame between the game's updates, keeping the cave text and the Item Counter's own drawing, so a line shows as soon as you pick the item up.
- **Archipelago: items you receive in a cellar are given there.** You no longer have to climb out of a dungeon's item cellar: received items arrive at once, as in the rest of the dungeon, and the "EXIT CELLAR FOR ITEMS" line is gone.
- **Archipelago: items you receive in a cave are announced at once.** Items still arrive as you leave (shops and gift caves pick their wares when you walk in, and the sword caves check your hearts), but each one's line ("GOT RAFT", "BOW FROM GOB") now shows right away, followed by "EXIT CAVE TO RECEIVE", instead of one "EXIT CAVE FOR ITEMS". The lines are not shown again when you walk out.
- **The compass marks its level's Triforce room again with Add Maps/Compasses or Add Triforces to the Item Pool on** (found in testing). With either setting on, the compasses of levels 1-7 pointed off the status bar's map, wherever the compass was found. Each compass now marks its level's original Triforce room, as without these settings, in Archipelago and single-player seeds alike. Seeds made with either setting on the earlier 2.0.2 test builds need to be regenerated; other seeds are unchanged.
- **With Add Triforces or Add Maps/Compasses to the Item Pool on, the compass's marker blinks until the marked room's item is taken.** The compass marks its level's original Triforce room, but its marker stopped blinking once that level's Triforce piece was held, wherever you found it, even while the room still held an item. It now stops when that room's item is taken (or, where the piece stood in an item cellar, the cellar's), whatever the room holds, and holding the piece no longer changes it. Level 9's marker blinks as before. ROMs with either setting on change; every other ROM is unchanged, byte for byte.
- **With Add Triforces to the Item Pool on, the room each level's compass marks never holds Bombs, 5 Rupees or a Key.** The compass marks its level's original Triforce room (or the item cellar where the piece stood), and with Add Triforces on that spot can hold another item. It could be bombs, 5 rupees or a key, or in Archipelago another player's filler, so the compass pointed at almost nothing. Now it always holds a real item, in single-player and Archipelago seeds alike. Single-player seeds change only with Add Triforces, Item Shuffle: Full and Shuffle Minor Dungeon Drops all on; Archipelago games with Add Triforces may place items differently; every other seed is unchanged.
- **Hints never point to a Triforce piece, map or compass** (found in testing). With Add Triforces or Add Maps/Compasses to the Item Pool on, a hint could say which level a Triforce piece waits in, that one needs the Ladder, or that the elder has the map; a hint naming another player's item never names another ZORA player's "Level 3 Triforce" either. These hints now name other items, the White Sword Cave's holder says "THE ITEM" when the cave holds one, and with Add Maps/Compasses on, level 9's people no longer say who guards its compass. Level 9's "YOU NEED EIGHT TRIFORCE PIECES" stays. Seeds with either setting on change; other seeds are unchanged.
- **The title screen and the ending no longer crash with Add Triforces or Add Maps/Compasses to the Item Pool on** (found in testing). The title screen froze in its list of treasures, about the keys, and the ending crashed when Zelda was rescued, because the game drew their compass, map and Triforce through the new per-level drawing, which their screens can't use. They now draw them as the game always has. Seeds made with either setting on the earlier 2.0.2 test builds need to be regenerated. Other single-player seeds are unchanged; every Archipelago ROM's bytes change (the drawing code is in all of them), but games without these settings play the same.
- **Item Counter: going down a level's stairs, the counter keeps the overworld's count until the level is on screen** (found in testing). About half the time it switched to the level's count as Link stepped into the entrance, while he was still walking down the stairs on the overworld. Leaving a level already kept the level's count until the overworld was on screen. Only seeds with the Item Counter on change.
- **The level numbers beside Triforce pieces, maps and compasses show with Music: Random** (reported by players on consoles). With Add Triforces or Add Maps/Compasses to the Item Pool on, a ROM with Music: Random never showed the numbers, on any console or emulator and with either release of the game, because the music engine moved the game's screen-update code without ZORA's change to it. It now keeps the change. Only Music: Random ROMs that can show these numbers change: single-player ROMs with either setting on, and Archipelago ROMs; seeds without Music: Random are unchanged.
- **Archipelago: the Magical Sword Cave never asks for more than 12 hearts.** With Randomize Magical Sword off and Change Sword Hearts on, the cave could ask for 13 or 14, one heart more than some seeds give, and since Archipelago counts that cave, everyone's generation stopped (about 1 seed in 100 with random settings). As single player already does with Randomize Magical Sword on, Archipelago now caps the cave at 12 whatever that setting says: the Multiworld tab and the YAML no longer offer 13 or 14, and a YAML (or the page's default flags) asking for more is read as 12 with a warning. Single-player seeds are unchanged.

## Music engine

Music engine: lozft by TheRealQuantam (MPL 2.0), using bhop by zeta0134 (MIT-0). ZORA's build of it is from https://github.com/TheRealQuantam/lozft, commit 44b1bb5, the source of the lozft code this release carries.
