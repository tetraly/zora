This guide takes you from the YAML you exported on ZORA's page to playing ZORA in a multiworld. You don't need to have used Archipelago before.

What Archipelago is

Archipelago is a multiworld randomizer. Several players, each playing their own game, share one item pool: the items of every game are shuffled across all of the games. When you pick up an item in ZORA, it may belong to another player, in another game, and the Archipelago server sends it to them. Their finds can be yours in the same way, and they arrive in your ZORA game while you play.

A multiworld can mix any games Archipelago supports, and it can have several ZORA players.

In ZORA:

  • Your own items look and work as usual. An item that belongs to another player looks like a single rupee, or like the Archipelago logo if you choose it on ZORA's page.
  • Items sent to you arrive during normal play, never in the middle of a screen scroll, on the item screen, or while text is showing.
  • Your goal is to rescue Zelda. The server is told when you do.

The files you'll handle

FileWhat it is
zora.apworldZORA's world for Archipelago. Install it once per ZORA version.
<your name>.yamlYour settings, exported from ZORA's page.
AP_<numbers>.zipThe generated game. The organizer hosts it.
AP_<numbers>_P<n>_<your name>.apzoraYour patch: about 1 KB, which is normal. It holds only the recipe for your game, no game data. The Archipelago Launcher builds your ROM from it.
AP_<numbers>_P<n>_<your name>.nesYour playable ZORA game, built by the Launcher next to the .apzora. Don't share it.

1. What you need

  • A computer running Windows or Linux to play. BizHawk, the emulator, doesn't run on a Mac. (A Mac can still generate and host a game.)
  • Archipelago 0.6.8, from its releases page. On Windows, run the installer; on Linux, use the AppImage or the archive.
  • BizHawk 2.9, 2.10 or 2.11, from BizHawk's site. On Windows, run BizHawk's prerequisites installer first.
  • zora.apworld for your ZORA version. ZORA's page links it in its multiworld steps: it's the file attached to that version's release on ZORA's GitHub releases page. Use the same ZORA version as the page you exported your YAML from.
  • Your own The Legend of Zelda (USA) ROM, as a .nes file. Either US release works:
    • the first release (often labeled PRG0);
    • the second release (PRG1, sometimes labeled Rev 1 or Rev A);
    • either one with modified graphics, such as a custom sprite set.

    Other versions (European, Japanese) and other hacks aren't recognized. You need the ROM to play, and the organizer needs one to generate. Players don't need the same release: each player's game is built from their own ROM.

2. Install the ZORA world

  1. Open the Archipelago Launcher.
  2. Click Install APWorld and choose zora.apworld. (Double-clicking the file works too, on systems where Archipelago is installed.)
  3. Close and reopen the Launcher.

Everyone who generates or plays ZORA in a game must use the same zora.apworld: a patch can only be built by the world that generated it. Players of other games don't need it.

To update to a newer ZORA version, install its zora.apworld the same way: it replaces the old one. A game already generated keeps needing the version that generated it.

3. Your YAML

ZORA's page, in its Multiworld (Archipelago) mode, exported your YAML: your player name, every option, and how your game looks and sounds (tunic and heart colors, music, the word before each level's number, and more). Give it to whoever is organizing the game.

  • Player names must all be different within a game, 16 characters at most.
  • To change your settings, go back to ZORA's page, change them, and export again. The page only lets you choose what Archipelago supports, and it refuses combinations ZORA can't make, with the reason.

4. Generate the game (the organizer)

One person, the organizer, gathers every player's YAML and generates the game. The organizer needs Archipelago and zora.apworld installed, even if they don't play ZORA themselves, and a Zelda ROM.

  1. In the Launcher, click Browse Files and open the Players folder. Move out any YAMLs from other games, and put in every player's YAML: any number of players and games.
  2. Back in the Launcher, click Generate.
  3. The first time, Archipelago asks for your Zelda ROM ("The Legend of Zelda (USA) ROM File"). Pick your .nes file. Archipelago keeps a copy of it, named after the first release, whichever release it is: that's expected.
  4. The game appears in the output folder as AP_<numbers>.zip.

If ZORA refuses the options, the window says which options conflict. That only happens with a hand-edited YAML: fix it, or export it again from ZORA's page, and generate again.

5. Host the game (the organizer)

Choose one:

  • On archipelago.gg (the easiest): go to archipelago.gg, choose Host Game, and upload AP_<numbers>.zip. The room page shows the address players connect to, for example archipelago.gg:38281. ZORA doesn't need to be installed on the site.
  • On your own computer: in the Launcher, click Host and choose the zip. Players outside your home network need port 38281 forwarded to your computer.

Then send each ZORA player their .apzora (inside the zip, named after their player name) and the room's address. Players of other games get whatever their game uses.

6. Play

Don't open the .apzora in BizHawk. It isn't a ROM, so BizHawk won't recognize it. The Archipelago Launcher turns it into one.

  1. Open the Archipelago Launcher and drag your .apzora onto its window. (Or click Open Patch and choose it.)
  2. The first time, the Launcher asks for your Zelda ROM, then for EmuHawk.exe in your BizHawk folder.
  3. The Launcher builds your game as a .nes file next to the .apzora. BizHawk opens with it, along with its Lua Console (leave that open), and the BizHawk Client opens.
  4. In the BizHawk Client, type the room's address if it isn't filled in, and click Connect. You never type a player name: your game carries it.
  5. The client says ZORA world <version>: recognized the ROM for ..., then that you're connected. Start a new file (or load yours) and play.

Next time, after closing everything, do the same again: drag the .apzora onto the Launcher. It builds the same game, and your save is kept, because BizHawk keeps it with the game's file name.

If BizHawk doesn't open by itself: start EmuHawk, choose File → Open ROM and pick the .nes next to your .apzora. Then choose Tools → Lua Console, Script → Open Script, and pick connector_bizhawk_generic.lua from Archipelago's data/lua folder. Finally, open the BizHawk Client from the Launcher.

Good to know while playing

  • Items that look like a single rupee (the flashing one) belong to another player. Picking one up sends it to them, and gives you one rupee. With Other players' items look like set to the Archipelago logo on ZORA's page, they show the logo instead and give you nothing; shops then sell a fairy where the magical shield was.
  • Items sent to you wait until normal play, then arrive one at a time, each with the sound of an item appearing. (On ZORA's page, Sound when an item arrives chooses that sound, the sound of picking up an item, or none.) The client's log lists each one, for example ZORA: gave received item #3, Raft.
  • Nothing is reported or given on the title or file-select screens: the client waits until you're playing.
  • To save: with the default Death-warp setting, open the item screen (Start), then hold Up and press A on controller 1. (If you changed Death-warp on ZORA's page, use the buttons you chose.) Losing all your hearts also brings up the menu. Choose SAVE.
  • Some items stay in your own game: rupees, hearts, fairies, maps, compasses, Triforce pieces and the clock are never sent between players. They stay where ZORA puts them. With Allow Triforces in Stairways on, each level's Triforce may be in any of that level's item spots, on a room's floor or down a stairway; the spoiler log lists where each one is.
  • Heart containers from Take Any caves stay in your game and aren't reported. Those caves aren't Archipelago locations; the heart containers in boss rooms and by the coast are.
  • With Progressive Items on, Archipelago names the upgrades by their line: Sword Upgrade, Candle Upgrade, Arrow Upgrade, Ring Upgrade and Boomerang Upgrade. Each one gives the next level of that item.
  • In-game hints name only your own items; the others say ANOTHER PLAYER'S ITEM. Archipelago's !hint command, typed in the client, covers the rest.

7. If something goes wrong

BizHawk won't open my .apzora, or it's only about 1 KB.
That's expected: drag it onto the Archipelago Launcher instead (step 6).

Dragging the .apzora onto the Launcher does nothing.
Check that zora.apworld is installed (step 2), then close and reopen the Launcher. Or use Open Patch.

Archipelago refuses my Zelda ROM ("ROM not recognized").
ZORA accepts the US releases of The Legend of Zelda (PRG0 or PRG1), with or without modified graphics. Other versions and other hacks aren't recognized. A ROM that was already randomized won't work either: use an unmodified one.

The client says "Waiting to connect to BizHawk...".
BizHawk's Lua Console must be open and running connector_bizhawk_generic.lua (see "If BizHawk doesn't open by itself" in step 6).

The client says "No handler was found for this game".
BizHawk has a plain Zelda ROM loaded, not your ZORA game. Load the .nes built next to your .apzora.

Generating says an option or value isn't known.
The YAML was exported from another ZORA version than the installed zora.apworld. Export it again from the page of the version you installed, or install the matching zora.apworld.

Generating says Shuffle Wood Sword is not offered.
ZORA's Archipelago logic assumes the wooden sword from the start, so this flag is always off there. Remove shuffle_wood_sword from the YAML, or set it to off.

The client says the game was generated by another ZORA world version, or the ROM was made by another recipe format.
Install the zora.apworld the game was generated with. Everyone in a game uses the same one.

An item doesn't arrive.
Items wait for normal play: close the item screen, finish the scroll or the text. The client's log says what it gave. If one still doesn't come, please report it.

Reporting a problem.
Ask in the ZORA Discord. It helps to include:

  • your YAML and the game's name (AP_<numbers>);
  • a screenshot of the BizHawk Client window;
  • the newest BizHawkClient file in Archipelago's logs folder (on Windows, C:\ProgramData\Archipelago\logs);
  • a BizHawk savestate from just before the problem, if you have one;
  • the spoiler log from inside the zip, if the organizer can share it (it reveals where every item is).
